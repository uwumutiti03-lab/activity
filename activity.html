Microcontrollers and Microprocessors

Comparison Table

Device Meaning Main Function Internet Connection Examples of Uses
Microcontroller A small computer on one chip. Controls electronic devices and sensors. Depends on the model. Washing machines, alarms and robots.
Microprocessor A processor that executes computer instructions. Processes data and runs complex programs. Usually through additional hardware. Desktop computers and laptops.
ESP32 A powerful microcontroller with wireless features. Controls devices and connects to the internet. Wi-Fi and Bluetooth. Smart home security and IoT projects.
ESP8266 A Wi-Fi-enabled microcontroller. Controls devices and communicates over Wi-Fi. Wi-Fi. Smart lights and remote monitoring.
Arduino Uno A development board based on the ATmega328P microcontroller. Controls electronic components using programmed instructions. No built-in Wi-Fi or Bluetooth. LEDs, sensors and automatic systems.

Conclusion

A microcontroller is designed to control electronic devices, while a microprocessor is designed to process complex instructions. ESP32 and ESP8266 are microcontroller-based boards with Wi-Fi. Arduino Uno is a development board used for learning electronics and building different projects.
